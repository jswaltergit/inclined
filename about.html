Our story

Decades in the Making

1981

Fu Associates

Inclined’s roots go back more than four decades to 1981, when technologist and public servant Edward Fu founded Fu Associates to help federal agencies solve hard operational problems. That work put our team inside some of the most complex programs in government, building the systems, data pipelines, and processes that let those programs run.

2024+

Advancing the Vision

In 2024, former public servant and technology executive Lena Trudeau acquired and relaunched the firm as Inclined, a woman-owned small business built on the same foundation of embedded, real-world expertise, focused on operational intelligence for programs that must perform at scale.

Expert in the Loop

Technology Guided by Deep Domain Expertise

Expert in the Loop is the philosophy that underpins everything we do. We believe the greatest outcomes are achieved when guided by experienced professionals with deep domain expertise. Technology is a powerful accelerator, but expertise is the foundation for sound judgment, creating trust and lasting value.
