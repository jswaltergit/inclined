A Different Kind of Consulting Firm

Inclined is small but mighty, a team of intellectually curious problem-solvers who genuinely care about making essential government programs work. We believe the best outcomes happen when talented people are trusted, supported, and united by a shared mission.

Why Inclined

15+Years average employee tenure

Our average employee tenure exceeds 15 years, a rare number in this industry, and a sign of what it’s like to work here. We invest in long-term relationships, both with our clients and with each other. We’re not a firm that rotates people through projects. We’re a team that stays with the problem until it’s solved.

What We Look For

We look for people who dig into hard problems and don’t let go until they’re solved, engineers, analysts, data scientists, policy experts, designers, project leaders, and operational specialists who want their work to matter. We hire and develop experts with the judgment, curiosity, and depth to interpret results, recognize risk, and make sound decisions. If you’re motivated by the mission and energized by the challenge, you’ll fit right in.

What You'll Find Here

Meaningful Work

Support critical government programs that improve healthcare, strengthen public services, and help agencies operate more effectively.

Exceptional People

Work alongside experienced professionals who are generous with their knowledge, collaborative in their approach, and committed to helping each other succeed.

Real Responsibility

You'll have opportunities to contribute, lead, and make a meaningful difference from the beginning, working on projects that demand creativity, curiosity, and continuous learning.

A Culture of Partnership

The way we work with our clients reflects the way we work with each other, with respect, transparency, collaboration, and trust.
